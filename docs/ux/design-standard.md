# MoHoLocal resident experience standard

Updated November 6, 2026. This is the design acceptance standard for the relaunch, not a claim that competitors have been beaten or that user testing has occurred.

The resident should be able to choose a city, describe a job, compare plausible providers and make contact with minimal effort. Facebook remains useful for conversations and personal recommendations; MoHoLocal adds structured, retrievable service information and documented freshness.

- **Task first:** home and city pages offer specific home-service choices. Service-area evidence allows a provider based elsewhere to appear without creating duplicate city listings.
- **Trust in context:** every information check shows its date, source and exact scope. Approved listings, owner claims and website checks mean different things. Avoid unsupported popularity, quality, licensing and availability claims.
- **Useful comparison:** show job fit, service geography, contact actions and missing information before decorative content. Empty shortlists explain the coverage gap and offer broader browsing.
- **Mobile access:** visible navigation targets at least 44px high, readable text, labeled search, visible keyboard focus, skip link and Escape dismissal for menus. No horizontal page overflow at 390px. Test narrower and larger sizes during resident testing.
- **Working participation:** saved submission and claim receipts, moderated publication and clear outcomes. Pet reports need visible dates and honest status; linked-reporter status confirmation and reversible archive are deployed; automatic 30-day public expiry and renewal are deployed.
- **Honest failure:** unavailable data must not look like zero listings. Give a recovery path, preserve entered searches, and distinguish loading, error and empty results.

Pilot tasks: find a plumber serving your city; compare two cleaners; check what was verified; find a provider's phone; submit a correction; locate recent pet reports. Observe completion time, wrong turns, contact attempts and confidence without coaching. Proposed gate: 15 of 20 residents find two plausible providers within two minutes, with denominators and failures recorded. No resident test has yet been run. Current category choice is at least three providers per focus city; the broader 30–40-provider pilot inventory target remains open.

Next design work: service-specific job details, additional current, rights-cleared city photography, legacy Best Of pages, notification usability, pet-status and renewal usability, real empty-search recovery, and resident observation. Keep roadmap, backlog and sprint state aligned with deployed and verified behavior.

Footer follow-up: four launch-city links come from the shared relaunch configuration, use a consistent neutral style with readable contrast, and have at least 44px touch targets. The footer no longer promotes South Bay coverage; existing other-city routes/data are preserved.

Photo-led homepage acceptance: exact-area imagery with a confirmed 2026 capture date, creator/source/license links and visible captions. Current accepted source is November 4, 2026 Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery, explicitly labeled satellite views with 10 m source resolution and the required modified-data notice. These are not street photographs or upcoming event photos. Older archival images and generated regional scenes are rejected for publication. Current 2026 street-level photos remain pending capture-date and reuse evidence. City selection must never relabel another city's imagery.

Seasonal cards require approved event rows, official source, explicit Pacific timezone and check date. Known end time expires the upcoming state at that instant; unknown end time remains through the local event date. Past events leave the homepage automatically while their approved detail remains readable as an archive. Do not interpret an events query error as an empty calendar.
