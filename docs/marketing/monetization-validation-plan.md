# Monetization validation plan

Owner: Growth, Community Marketing & Monetization Lead. Added November 8, 2026. Status: research / proposal only. No billing, outreach, advertising spend or paid placements activated.

## MON-01 — Validate willingness to pay before implementation

Start with one small, optional business offer after the core directory is usable. Candidates to compare: an enhanced owner-managed listing, an explicitly labeled sponsored placement, or a clearly labeled local sponsorship. These are hypotheses, not committed products or prices. Keep basic resident browsing and basic listings free for initial validation.

Acceptance:
- Define the business problem and measurable value of each offer; avoid charging simply to hide contact details or obtain corrections.
- Check comparable public offerings using dated sources before proposing a price. No competitor or market research completed in this document.
- Prepare a short owner interview / opt-in interest script and a decision threshold before any future explicitly authorized outreach. Record stated willingness separately from actual paid uptake. No fabricated demand.
- Estimate delivery and support costs from actual invoices/usage; model assumptions separately from measured costs. Compare gross revenue, direct costs, acquisition cost and retention; do not present directory traffic as profit.
- Define a small reversible test with duration, owner value, fulfillment, cancellation/refund process and explicit sponsorship disclosure before activation. Payment processor, billing and tax/accounting implementation remain unscoped until the offer is validated.
- Make a go / revise / stop decision with the evidence and sample limitations. Do not build subscriptions merely because competitors have them.

## Non-negotiable product rules

Payment does not buy website-check labels, owner verification, resident reviews, undisclosed priority or quality endorsements. Show sponsored items as sponsored and preserve useful organic discovery. An enhanced profile can describe features actually delivered; no promised leads, guaranteed rankings or confirmed business availability. Do not repurpose private resident messages or pet reports for advertising.

## Sequence

Resident launch acceptance → establish a real use/outcome baseline → prepare offer and interview materials → authorized opt-in validation → explicit decision on a small paid test → implement billing only if justified. SEO and Facebook drafts continue without publication.
