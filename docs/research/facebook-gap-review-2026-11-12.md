## Primary platform context

[Meta October2024 local features announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/facebook-local-tab-messenger-communities-ai/) documents local groups/activities and event digests. It is a dated announcement, not proof every feature is currently available in every local group. [Meta November2025 group privacy update](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/11/facebook-update-group-admins-flexibility-protecting-member-privacy/) documents protection of prior private-group content; nothing in it authorizes importing residents’ posts or images. Do not rely on old Community Chats announcements as a current all-group capability claim.

## Product decision

Make MoHoLocal the dependable record and next-action layer people can use alongside existing groups. Largest unproven gap is genuine participation and return habit, not count of UI widgets. Prioritize functional Activity stream filters, exact task/city service discovery, real reply/notification acceptance and genuine reporter outcomes. Measure real useful contacts, questions receiving useful replies, corrections resolved, event actions and returning residents. Baseline and attribution required; no invented engagement, fake profiles, bot conversations or100kaddressable-market forecasts. These are recommendations/inferences from bounded observations and code, not representative demand prevalence.

No code for a separate social network, new stack, paid marketing, memberships or publication is authorized by this review. FB drafts remain unpublished; imagery tasks and genuine-reporter pilot remain open.
