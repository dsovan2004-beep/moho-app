## Follow-up scope

- Clearly labeled, owner-authorized business offers with end dates; sponsorship never implies information verification or quality endorsement.
- Opt-in resident holiday display submissions; private home locations/photos require the owner's deliberate publication choice. No imported Facebook addresses or private photos.
- Cultural festivals and other celebrations based on community submissions and organizer sources, without inferring residents' beliefs.
- Separate explicit city/season notification preferences; enabling private-message emails or push must not subscribe someone to seasonal promotions.
- Native saves/RSVPs only with real account actions; no invented popularity or attendance counters.

## Acceptance measures

Relevant event discovery, calendar saves, useful discussion replies, source corrections, local business contacts and repeat visits. Prefer a small useful pilot to an unmaintained calendar. Each published event needs organizer/source, city, date/timezone or an explicit unknown, venue, current participation link/cost if established, last check, expiry and moderation controls. Verify responsive, keyboard, empty, cancelled and expired states before launch.
